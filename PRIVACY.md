# Privacy and terms: AutoPosterTAE

AutoPosterTAE is a small tool that posts The Ambience Experience's own videos
(short clips from youtube.com/@theambienceexperience) to The Ambience
Experience's own Instagram account. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one
else can sign in to it.

## What it uses

- An access token for The Ambience Experience's Instagram account, stored as an
  encrypted secret in this repository and used only to publish posts, read the
  account's own posts and comments, and reply on its own posts.
- Public comments left on The Ambience Experience's posts, read to answer
  questions there. They are not stored, sold or shared.

It collects no data about anyone else, keeps no user database, uses no cookies
or tracking, and shares nothing with third parties.

## Terms

The tool exists only to publish The Ambience Experience's own work to its own
account. It may be changed or switched off at any time.

## Data deletion

There is no stored personal data to delete. To have a comment reply removed, or
for any question, send a direct message to The Ambience Experience on Instagram.
Removing the app under Instagram, Settings, Apps and websites ends its access
immediately.
